# Date night ideas for Jordan and Krysia

Running ledger for the weekly date-night generator. Source of truth for
what's already been suggested, so nothing repeats. Mirrored into a Google
Doc for easy phone access ("Life OS - Autism-Friendly Date Night Ideas").

## Context

- **Who:** Jordan (Mitcham) and Krysia (Ealing), London, UK.
- **Profile:** both Autistic. Neither drinks alcohol, so don't suggest
  pubs, bars, or alcohol-centred venues.
- **Style:** autism-friendly, low-sensory, calm. "Meet in the middle"
  between Mitcham and Ealing, or easily reached by public transport from
  both. Occasionally further afield only if exceptionally tranquil or
  interesting.
- **Interests:** Pokemon, Disney, Moomins, Museums, Parks, Nature, Coffee,
  Fine Art, Architecture, History.
- **Output format:** WhatsApp-friendly message with links and prices
  listed, plus a "text infographic" per idea, a code block covering
  Sensory Profile, Highlights, and Quick Facts, easy to screenshot and
  share. British English. No em dashes.
- **Cadence:** 3 new ideas per week, never repeating a venue already listed
  below. A weekly Routine (Monday mornings) handles this automatically.

## Ideas suggested so far

### Week 1
- The Cartoon Museum (Fitzrovia): £8.50/adult. Nostalgic comic art,
  retro animation, low-stimulation galleries.
  https://www.cartoonmuseum.org/
- The Garden Museum (Lambeth): £16.00/adult. Converted medieval church,
  silent courtyard garden, quiet cafe.
  https://www.gardenmuseum.org.uk/
- Queen Mary's Rose Garden (Regent's Park): FREE. 12,000+ roses,
  geometric circular beds, peaceful park benches.
  https://www.royalparks.org.uk/visit/parks/regents-park/queen-marys-gardens

### Week 2
- Japan House London (Kensington): FREE. Minimalist design, uncluttered
  layouts, quiet tea/coffee stand.
  https://www.japanhouselondon.uk/
- Chiswick House & Gardens (Chiswick): FREE (gardens). 65 acres of
  grounds, quiet lake, historic conservatory, outdoor coffee.
  https://chiswickhouseandgardens.org.uk/
- The Postal Museum & Mail Rail (Farringdon): around £18.00/adult. Visual
  communications, timed entry, 15-min underground mini-train ride.
  https://www.postalmuseum.org/

### Week 3
- The Wallace Collection (Marylebone): FREE. Historic townhouse, fine
  art, light-filled glazed courtyard cafe.
  https://www.wallacecollection.org/
- Fulham Palace & Gardens (Fulham): FREE. Tudor courtyard, botanical
  walled garden, quiet drawing room cafe.
  https://www.fulhampalace.org/
- Wellcome Collection (Euston): FREE. Science and art museum, clear
  layouts, dedicated quiet seating.
  https://wellcomecollection.org/

### Week 4
- Museum of Brands (Notting Hill): £14.00/adult. Linear single-direction
  "Time Tunnel", retro toys, secluded garden cafe.
  https://museumofbrands.com/
- Leighton House (Kensington): £12.25 to £14.00/adult. Victorian artist
  studio, mosaic Arab Hall, indoor fountain, bright garden cafe.
  https://www.rbkc.gov.uk/museums/leighton-house
- The Foundling Museum (Bloomsbury): £13.50 online. Touching history,
  classical music, quiet rooms, opens onto Brunswick Square.
  https://foundlingmuseum.org.uk/

### Week 5
- Sir John Soane's Museum (Holborn): FREE. Dimly lit historic townhouse,
  capped visitor entry, antiquities.
  https://www.soane.org/
- Horniman Museum Gardens (Forest Hill): FREE. 16 acres of gardens, herb
  displays, Victorian conservatory, scenic views.
  https://www.horniman.ac.uk/
- Fenton House & Garden (Hampstead): around £8.50/adult. 17th-century
  merchant house, 300-year-old orchard, sunken walled garden.
  https://www.nationaltrust.org.uk/visit/london/fenton-house-and-garden

### Week 6, 2026-08-16
- Pitzhanger Manor & Gallery (Ealing): £12.00/adult from 29 July 2026
  (£13.20 with Gift Aid), free for Pitzhanger members, under-16s, and
  carers. Sir John Soane's own country villa, set inside Walpole Park (a
  free public park around it).
  https://www.pitzhanger.org.uk/
- Gunnersbury Park & Museum (Ealing/Hounslow/Hammersmith border): FREE.
  186 acres of parkland plus a free museum in a historic mansion, roughly
  the literal midpoint between Mitcham and Ealing.
  https://www.visitgunnersbury.org/
- Chelsea Physic Garden (Chelsea): around £13.50 to £15/adult (check exact
  price at booking, seasonal offers apply). England's oldest botanic
  garden, walled and sheltered, quiet even when central London is busy.
  https://www.chelseaphysicgarden.co.uk/

## Format template for new entries

```
Venue name (Area)
Sensory Profile: quiet/busy, lighting, noise, crowding notes
Highlights: 2 to 3 things specific to this venue
Quick Facts:
   Price: £X / adult
   Hours: days, times
   Getting there: rough transport note from Mitcham/Ealing
   Link: official site
```
